Aircraft operators around the world consistently face communication challenges in areas void of cell and radio service. TextAny Pilot is a cost-effective, Iridium satellite-based solution that aids in safe aircraft operation. The device simply connects by WiFi to any smartphone, tablet, or laptop and requires no special software or certification.
